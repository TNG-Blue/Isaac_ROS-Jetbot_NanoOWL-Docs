    Ground rules¶

    • Participation is governed by the Code of Conduct.
    • First-time contributors sign the CLA by commenting I have read and agree to the CLA on their first pull request.
    • Changes to control, estimation or firmware code carry their derivation, units, sample rate and stability argument; see the Control Design Standard.
